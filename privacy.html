Melodrop

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Who we are

Melodrop is a music video channel. Melodrop Publisher is the tool we use to post our own videos to our own Melodrop accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Threads. Only the channel owner uses it.

What we access

When the channel owner connects a Melodrop account, the platform gives Melodrop Publisher an access token. With it we:

  • read basic account info (username, display name, profile picture) to confirm the right account is connected
  • upload videos and captions that the channel owner has approved
  • read view, like and comment counts for our own posts so we can see what works

We do not collect information about viewers or any other users. We do not read private messages.

How we store and use it

Access tokens and post statistics are kept on our private server and used only to run the Melodrop accounts. We do not sell or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

YouTube

Melodrop Publisher uses YouTube API Services. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Melodrop Publisher's access to a Google account at any time at Google security settings.

TikTok, Instagram and Threads

Access can be removed at any time in each app's settings under connected or authorized apps. When access is removed, we delete the stored token.

Deleting data

We delete stored tokens and statistics when an account is disconnected, or within 30 days of a request sent to the email below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
